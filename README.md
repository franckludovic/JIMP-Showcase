## About

**JIMP** (Internship & Job Matcher Platform) is a cross-platform mobile application designed to streamline the process of finding and applying for internships and jobs.

The platform serves two types of users - **Job Seekers / Students** who browse listings, apply, track applications and communicate with recruiters; and **Companies / Recruiters** who post opportunities, review candidates, manage proposals, and hire with confidence.

Far from a simple job board, JIMP was designed as an **end-to-end career accelerator**, combining smart filtering, real-time application tracking, in-app messaging, document management and analytics - all in one seamless mobile experience.

## Why Use JIMP?

- **Find the right opportunity, faster** : Browse internships and jobs tailored to your profile, skills, and interests with an intuitive search and category system.
- **Apply and track in real time** : Submit applications directly in the app and follow every step of the recruitment process live.
- **Connect directly with companies** : Message recruiters, respond to proposals and manage your ongoing contracts - no middleman needed.
- **Stand out and grow** : Upload your CV, build your profile, collect reviews, and gain visibility with the companies that matter.

---

## Access Formulas & User Types

The platform is tailored to accommodate different profiles and needs:

- **Job Seeker / Student Profile** : Full access to listings, search, applications, saves, notifications, proposals and in-app chat.
- **Company / Recruiter Profile** : Access to the company dashboard, applicant management, application reviews and direct messaging.
- **Dual Navigation** : Each user type benefits from a dedicated navigation experience adapted to their workflow.
- **Personalization** : Profile customization, user-type selection at onboarding, and preference-based recommendations.
